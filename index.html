See what your NFL parlay is really worth

Clearline Parlay Lab is an NFL parlay analyzer built around one idea: a ticket is only as good as the chance it actually hits. We turn sportsbook prices into plain probabilities so you can judge a parlay before you bet it.

Most parlay slips show you one big number, the payout. That number is easy to love and hard to evaluate. It says nothing about how often the ticket should win, and it hides how quickly the odds shrink as you add legs. Our analyzer puts the missing half of the picture next to the payout, so you can compare the two.

The short version. Every leg you add multiplies the chance of losing. A ticket with three legs that are each a coin flip wins only 12.5 percent of the time. Whether that is a good bet depends on what the book pays, and that is exactly what we help you check.

How the analyzer thinks

Our method has three steps, and you can follow each one by hand. First we convert the price on every leg into the probability the sportsbook is implying. Second we remove the built-in margin, often called the vig, to estimate a fair probability. Third we combine the legs and compare the result to the payout. If the payout is bigger than the fair odds would require, the ticket has positive expected value. If it is smaller, you are paying a premium to play.

We are not in the business of telling you what to bet. We would rather you leave knowing why a ticket costs what it costs.
